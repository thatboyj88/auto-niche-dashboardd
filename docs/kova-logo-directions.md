# Kova logo directions

The production identity uses the approved **K Orb**: a luminous capital K
inside a glossy blue/violet glass orb on an opaque near-black background.

Future exploration may consider these directions without changing the current
production mark:

1. **Kova Shield** — geometric K with a shield silhouette for safety and risk.
2. **Kova Orb** — glowing geometric K with a minimal AI-oriented treatment.
3. **Kova Pulse** — angular K with a restrained market/pulse accent.
4. **Kova Chart** — K formed from market movement, kept minimal.
5. **Kova Eye** — abstract observing eye with a K at its centre.
6. **KV Monogram** — combined K and V for a broader technology identity.

No alternate direction is implemented by this note.
